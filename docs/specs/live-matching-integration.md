# Live matching and activation

> EXPERIMENTAL — TEST NETWORK ONLY — NOT A FINANCIAL PRODUCT OR SERVICE — NO REAL FUNDS — AS IS, NO WARRANTY, NO LIABILITY — USE AT YOUR OWN RISK

The matcher reads only active, retained, authorized listings. It excludes synthetic rows from organic operation, deleted or revoked owners, expired offers, unavailable capacity, and any input whose evidence cannot be validated.

Compatible intents are grouped against ordered seller tiers using product, region, time window, quantity, unit ceiling, settlement preferences, outstanding capacity, and binding rules. A prepared result is private and tentative. It is not publication, reveal authorization, a commitment, or settlement.

Before activation, the seller's enrolled capacity endpoint must pass the signed capacity protocol for the exact proposed allocation. Publication binds the public batch hash and the private manifest digest. Activated roster pages are immutable and paginated; no partial or mixed-version activation is accepted.

Only manifest participants may use the staged reveal, agreement, commitment, attestation, and bilateral handoff routes. Publication and reveal never authorize real funds, mainnet, supplier outreach, or acceptance of a commercial contract.
